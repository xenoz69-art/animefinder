Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Introduction

AnimeFinder ("we") respects the privacy of its users ("you"). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have when using the name- and image-based (screenshot) anime search service on this site.

Data We Collect

  • Search keywords — the anime title or keywords you type into the name search feature.
  • Images/screenshots — image files you upload for the image-based search feature are sent to a third-party service (trace.moe) solely to identify anime scenes, and are not stored permanently on our servers.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser and device type, which may be logged automatically for security and performance improvements.

How Data Is Used

Search data and images you submit are used solely to process search requests through third-party APIs, namely AniList and trace.moe. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with other parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

AnimeFinder uses public APIs from AniList and trace.moe to provide search results. Use of these services is subject to each provider's own privacy policy, which we recommend reading separately.

Cookies & Local Storage

This site may use browser local storage only to save display preferences, and not to track your personal identity on other sites.

Data Security

We strive to keep data secure through reasonable technical practices. However, no method of data transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You have the right not to use the image upload feature if you don't want your images processed by third-party services. You can also reach us via the Contact page with questions about your data.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. Changes take effect once published on this page.
