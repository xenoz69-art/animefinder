About AnimeFinder

Finding anime should be as easy as remembering it.

Our Story

AnimeFinder was born from a simple problem every anime fan has faced: seeing a scene without knowing its title, or forgetting the name of an anime watched years ago. We built AnimeFinder as a fast search tool combining name-based search and image-based search in one place.

What We Do

  • Search by Name — browse the complete anime database through AniList, from popular titles to hidden gems.
  • Search by Image — upload a screenshot of a scene and let trace.moe match it to the right anime, complete with episode and timestamp.
  • Explore Trends — track trending, popular, and the latest anime right from the homepage.

Powered By

AnimeFinder is built on two trusted services in the anime community: AniList for anime data and database, and trace.moe for image-based scene search and matching.
